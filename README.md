# About my Favorite Movie, Top Gun Maverick

![TGM](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/topgun/images/6/66/Top_Gun_Maverick_Poster_2.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20191215142513)

**Top Gun: Maverick** is a critically acclaimed 2022 American action drama film directed by Joseph Kosinski. Serving as the sequel to the iconic 1986 film Top Gun, it features Tom Cruise reprising his legendary role as naval aviator Pete "Maverick" Mitchell. The movie is highly celebrated for its groundbreaking use of real aircraft, practical effects, and immersive in-cockpit IMAX camera rigs rather than relying heavily on CGI.
